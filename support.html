About the app

Krypto Math Kids is a mathematics learning game for children. Kids practise arithmetic through playful games and learn mental-math techniques — including the ×11 "magic" trick, where they become a maths wizard by splitting numbers apart.

Common questions

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. Krypto Math Kids works fully offline. Nothing is uploaded or downloaded while your child plays.
Does it collect any information about my child?
No. The app collects no personal information at all — no name, no email, no account, no tracking. Game progress is saved only on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Where is my child's progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the app removes all saved progress. Progress does not sync between devices.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
The current version has no advertising and no in-app purchases.
My child is stuck or the app isn't working correctly.
Try closing and reopening the app first. If a problem continues, email us at info@acuitygen.com with your device model and iOS version, and a short description of what happened — it helps us fix it faster.
How do I suggest a feature or report feedback?
We'd love to hear from you. Send ideas and feedback to info@acuitygen.com.
