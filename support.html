❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What ages is Math Mastery designed for?

Math Mastery is designed for children ages 5-12. The games cover foundational math concepts through advanced mental math techniques, making it suitable for a wide range of skill levels.

Do I need to create an account?

No! Math Mastery works completely offline and doesn't require any account creation, login, or personal information. Your child can start learning immediately.

How is progress saved?

All progress (stars earned) is saved automatically on your device. Progress is preserved as long as the app remains installed on your device.

Can I reset my progress?

Yes! To reset all progress, simply uninstall and reinstall the app. This will clear all saved stars and start fresh.

Does the app work offline?

Yes! Math Mastery works completely offline. No internet connection is required to play any of the games.

Are there any in-app purchases or ads?

No. Math Mastery is completely free with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscription required. All 16 games are available immediately.

What devices are supported?

Math Mastery is available for iPhone and iPad running iOS 16.0 or later. It's optimized for iPad but works great on iPhone too.

The app is crashing or not working properly. What should I do?

Please try the following: 1) Make sure you have the latest version of the app installed, 2) Restart your device, 3) If the problem persists, please contact us with details about your device and the issue.

🎓 Learning Tips for Parents

💡 Start with Practice Games

If your child is new to the app, start with the 6 Practice Games to build foundational skills before moving to the technique hubs.

🎯 Embrace Wrong Answers

When your child gets an answer wrong, the app shows step-by-step explanations. These "teaching moments" are actually the most valuable part of learning!

🌟 Celebrate Progress

Every star earned represents a correct answer and a small victory. Celebrate these achievements to build your child's confidence in math.
