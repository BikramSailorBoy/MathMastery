Privacy Policy — Krypto Math Kids

Effective date: 18 August 2026 · Last updated: 18 August 2026

The short version: Krypto Math Kids collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party services. Your child's game progress is saved only on your own device and is never sent anywhere. If you delete the app, that data is gone.

About this app

Krypto Math Kids is a mathematics learning game designed for children. This policy explains — in plain language — how the app handles information. It is written to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including protections for children, and Apple's Kids Category requirements.

The app is operated by AcuityGen LLP ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at the email below.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, the app does not ask for, access, or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details
  • Dates of birth, age, or gender
  • Location data
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or usage analytics
  • Any information that could identify your child or your device

The app has no login, no user account, and no registration. It does not connect to the internet to send or receive personal data.

What is stored on your device

So your child doesn't lose their progress, the app saves a small amount of game data locally on your device only. This includes things like the number of stars earned, experience points, level, streak count, and which lessons have been completed. This is anonymous game state — it contains no personal information, it never leaves the device, and it is not accessible to us or anyone else. Deleting the app permanently removes it.

No third parties

Krypto Math Kids contains no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any information to anyone, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

This app is intended for children and is designed to be safe for them to use. Because we collect no personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or any age). No parental consent is required for data collection, because there is no data collection. Parents can use the app with full confidence that nothing about their child is gathered or transmitted.

International users (GDPR)

Since the app processes no personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, export, or delete on our servers — we hold none. Any information the app stores stays on your own device and is under your control. You can remove all of it at any time by deleting the app.

Purchases

The current version of Krypto Math Kids contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. If paid features are added in the future, all payments will be handled directly and securely by Apple through the App Store; we will never see or store your payment details, and any purchase will be placed behind an age-appropriate parental gate as required by Apple. This policy will be updated before any such feature is released.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices ever change, we will update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect in the app.
