Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

1. Introduction

Math Mastery ("the App") is an educational application designed for children. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices.

🔒 Our Commitment: Math Mastery is designed with privacy as a core principle. We believe children should be able to learn without being tracked or having their data collected.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require account creation, login, or any form of registration. All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage mechanisms and are not transmitted to any servers.

3. Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Total stars earned (game progress)
  • Game settings and preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Math Mastery is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal information from any users, no parental consent is required.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

6. Advertisements and In-App Purchases

The App does not contain any advertisements or in-app purchases. Your children can enjoy a completely ad-free learning experience.

7. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and we do not collect or transmit any personal information, your data is protected by your device's built-in security features.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. All game progress stored on your device can be reset by uninstalling and reinstalling the App.
